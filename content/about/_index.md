I'm Olivier, a systems engineer based in Lyon, France. About 25 years
in low-level software — C, C++, and now Rust — across Linux, FreeBSD,
macOS, and Windows.

## What I work on

I'm currently staff engineer on a long-lived Linux-based appliance
platform deployed in many thousands of production sites.
Most of my recent work is the unglamorous half of long-lived
software: security hardening, architecture modernization, and the
occasional performance overhaul when the hardware moves under us.

Before that, I co-founded two companies (network and
security services; distributed HTTP proxies for mobile
carriers), worked on embedded edge content delivery, and did a
stretch teaching to cumputer science students.

## What this blog is

A place where I write up technical things I find worth documenting —
sometimes from work, more often from side projects and experiments
I run for their own sake. The series on appliance security
came out of my day job; what comes next probably won't. Expect a
fairly broad range: Linux internals, security, Rust performance
work, and whatever else I happen to be digging into.
